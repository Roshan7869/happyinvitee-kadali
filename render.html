Our Story

We met at a cousin's engagement in Madurai — one of us lost a slipper outside the temple, the other found it. Six years, two cities and a great many filter coffees later, our families are lighting the lamp together.

सप्तपदी · saptapadi

Seven steps, seven promises

Our cloths tied together, we take seven steps around the sacred fire — and with each, a promise.

1of seven
  1. Step one

    For nourishment

    That our home will always have food to share, and no guest will leave it hungry.

  2. Step two

    For strength

    That we grow strong together — in body, in mind and in spirit.

  3. Step three

    For prosperity

    That we build our wealth honestly and spend it with kindness.

  4. Step four

    For happiness

    That we find joy in each other, and in each other's families.

  5. Step five

    For family

    That we raise our children with love, and care for our elders.

  6. Step six

    For every season

    That we stay together through every season, the gentle and the hard.

  7. Step seven

    For friendship

    That above all, we remain the best of friends — for this life and the next.
